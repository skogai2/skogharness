## Mistakes
- Offered "rename the skill in skogai" as a ready next item without first checking whether the two copies had diverged; they had, both ways.
- Used `git commit -F -` with a heredoc despite context_summary already recording that the gate false-positives on it.

## Learnings
- Before syncing two copies of a doc, diff them both ways; "the newer one" is rarely all on one side.
- `herdr --skill` regenerates herdr's reference for the installed version; never vendor it.
